Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

Who we are

Storygrain Creative is a creative marketing company based in Omaha, Nebraska. This policy covers storygrain.co, including the contact form and the Content Check.

What we collect

  • Information you send us. When you use the contact form or send your Content Check results, we receive your name, email, business name, your message or quiz answers, and how you found the page (for example, the ad or link you clicked).
  • Quiz answers you don't send. If you take the Content Check without sending your results, your answers stay in your browser. We don't receive them.
  • Basic site data. Our host and any advertising tools we use may collect standard technical information such as browser type, pages visited and general location.

How we use it

We use what you send to reply to you, understand what your business needs, and decide together whether we're a good fit. We may also use site data to understand which pages and ads are working.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We use a few service providers to run the site: Netlify (hosting and form handling), Google Fonts (typefaces) and, when we run ads, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) to measure ad results. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

Cookies and ad measurement

If we run Facebook or Instagram ads, the site may use the Meta Pixel to measure when someone completes the Content Check or sends their results. You can manage ad preferences in your Facebook and Instagram settings.

How long we keep it

We keep form submissions as long as needed to follow up and serve you as a client, then delete what we no longer need.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you sent us at any time. Email hello@storygrain.co and we'll take care of it.
